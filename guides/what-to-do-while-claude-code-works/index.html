Guide

What to do while Claude Code works

A coding agent can work for minutes without needing you. The useful move is not to supervise every line. Pick something shorter than the wait, keep notifications quiet, and let the agent ask when it genuinely needs input.

For a one-minute wait

Stay in the room. Look away from the screen, name the claim you will verify later, or decide what done means. A micro quest should not open another tab.

For five minutes or more

Choose one bounded activity: a chess sprint, a short walk, one relevant concept, or a complete click path through the feature being built. Stop when the activity ends, not when a feed runs out.

When Claude needs judgment

Taste and reality are better uses of a person than busywork. Give one sharp opinion, inspect the actual interface, or define the proof level the task requires.
