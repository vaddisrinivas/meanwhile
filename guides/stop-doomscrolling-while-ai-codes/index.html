Guide

Stop doomscrolling while AI codes

The problem is not taking a break. The problem is choosing a break with no stopping point. Coding-agent waits create small attention gaps, and an infinite feed is designed to expand into them. Use something finite instead.

Choose an edge

One puzzle sprint, one song, one random article, one glass of water, or one five-minute walk all tell you when they are over. That edge makes returning easy.

Keep short waits tabless

For less than two minutes, do not open a site at all. Look far away, notice three sounds, or write the acceptance line for the feature being built.

Do not optimize the break

A restorative or playful quest does not need tracking. No streak, completion button, or score is required unless the activity itself happens to be a game.
